	Weaknesses and Skill strengths: Most character should have at least one of each; stronger characters should generally have more weaknesses to compensate.
	
	Acheron's Alzheimer's:
	At the end of each turn, Acheron has a 1-5% chance of performing one of the following: losing 1 spell point, 'forgetting' an item or multiple items in his inventory, 'forgetting' 1 or more enemies, or having his vision reduced to 0 for 1 or more turns, even with items like a torch.
	He also collapses into a 'rest' state (inert, small hp and stamina gains at start of turn) whenever his mana points are reduced to 0.

	Tiny: 
	His relationships naturally improve with magic users and naturally degrade with everyone else.

	When killed (hp falls into the negative < than 20% of his health) Tiny's magic 'becomes unstable' and he blows apart, dealing damage to friend and foe alike. 8-10 'magic stones' are generated in any of the adjacent rooms and now represent his corpse. If any of the rooms have a pit in it, then the stones have a 50% chance of falling into the pit.
	If a pc with intelligence greater than 16 and/or available mana points can channel a certain amount of mana into each of the stones (hundreds of points, let's say), and can collect every stone into their inventory, then tiny can be reborn as a slightly weaker version of himself, let's say with -2 stats all around.

Goblin spider queen: can only play the game by herself, gestates eggs on her back to spawn minions that can fill specific roles, can climbs walls, spawn webs and spray acid, very powerful.
		Half-ogre barbarian: strong and slow neutral-aligned strength character.
		Mountain giant/earth golem: strong and slow good-aligned strength character, consumes rocks to regain hp and stamina, powerful but slow aoe abilities.
		Ancient troll: strong and slow evil-aligned strength character, naturally high hp regen, weak against fire.
		Hedge knight: neutral character, most versatile of the three knight characters, starts with level one of most weapon skills, decent stats.
		Crusader knight: good character, most tanky of the three knight characters, with morale boosting skills.
		Fallen knight: evil character, most dps of the three knight characters, with some small necromantic abilities and inflicts morale damage.
		Paladin: good, the anti-necromancer.
		Necromancer: evil, self explanatory.
		Ancient wizard: good, your quintessential wizard character.
		Priest: good, your quintessential priest character.
		Eleven archer: good, your quintessential archer character, some minor thief skills.
		Treasure hunter: neutral, your quintessential thief character, good at detecting and disarming traps, also increases the amount of loot that can be found on a level.
		Enchantress: Neutral or evil, medium level wizard character, specializes in buffs, debuffs, and summons.
		Big game hunter: neutral, ranged dps and disable.
		Assassin: neutral or evil, melee dps, disable, and stealth.
		Druid/shaman: neutral or good, all-around medium level wizard/support character, with healing abilities and some summons.
		Gnome artificer: neutral or good, specializes in golem summons.
		Mercenary/rogue mage: neutral or evil, a hybrid between a fighter and a mage.
		Sorcerer: evil mage, most dps and offensive spells of all the spell caster characters.
		Human ranger: neutral, hybrid archer/fighter, some thief skills.
